propose_plan_change — Propose a change to the work linked to this conversation, only when it already has an accepted plan. Arguments: changes, a list of changes, each with field (objective | owner_id | step_assignee), to_value (the new value; for owner_id and step_assignee only an id from the roster or the active agents provided) and step_id (only for step_assignee, when the phase must be picked). NOT for deadlines, constraints, the title or the agreement's collaborator: those change by calling propose_work, which revises the existing agreement. The preview is shown to the person: nothing is applied until they confirm.
