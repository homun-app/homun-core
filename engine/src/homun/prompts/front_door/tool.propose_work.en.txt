propose_work — Propose a work agreement from the person's request. The proposal waits for their confirmation: the engine starts nothing. Required arguments: title (short), objective (what must be achieved), output (the expected, visible result). Optional arguments: constraints, missing_information (what the person still owes you; a non-empty list when the request was vague), capability (general | synthesize | read_material | compare_csv | agent_run), suggested_agent_id (only an id among the active agents provided), new_agent (only when a brand-new profile is needed, with name and role), rationale, plan_steps (phases: title, capability, assignee as the display name of a roster person or empty, output_expected, expected_materials).
