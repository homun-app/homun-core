propose_work — Propose a work agreement from the person's request. The proposal waits for their confirmation: the engine starts nothing. Required arguments: title (short), objective (what must be achieved), output (the expected, visible result). Optional arguments: constraints, missing_information (what the person still owes you; a non-empty list when the request was vague), capability (general | synthesize | read_material | compare_csv | agent_run), suggested_agent_id (only an id among the active agents provided), new_agent (only when a brand-new profile is needed, with name and role), rationale, changed_fields (the ONLY fields this request changes compared with the work's already proposed or confirmed agreement: title | objective | output | constraints | capability | staffing | plan_steps — the engine keeps every other field; always declare it when you re-propose an agreement to change part of it), plan_steps (phases: title, capability, assignee ONLY the name of an active agent provided or of the proposed new_agent, otherwise empty — people named in the request who are not collaborators go in the phase title or the constraints, never in assignee; output_expected, expected_materials).
